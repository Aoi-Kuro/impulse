Getting Started!

    

      To get started, choose a nickname. It’s used to label your quiz attempts and forum posts and sync your stats across devices. Your stats are private , and your nickname is only visible when you make a forum post.

Exit this device?

    

      This only signs this nickname out on this device — the nickname stays
      claimed, your PIN keeps working, and any other device you've linked it to (phone/tablet/PC) is unaffected.
      To use it here again later, you'll need the nickname and its PIN — make sure you have the PIN saved somewhere
      before exiting.
